## Presenter Pronunciation & Technical Terms Guide

- **Jezero**: *YEH-zuh-roh* (Slavic for "lake").
- **Allison & McEwen**: *AL-ih-son and mik-YOO-en* (Planetary chronometry algorithms).
- **CTX DTM**: *C-T-X Digital Terrain Model* (Context Camera 20m elevation data).
- **CRISM**: *KRIZ-um* (Compact Reconnaissance Imaging Spectrometer for Mars).
- **MEDA**: *MEH-duh* (Mars Environmental Dynamics Analyzer on Perseverance).
- **RAD**: *Rad* (Radiation Assessment Detector on Curiosity).
- **BVAD**: *B-VAD* (Baseline Values and Assumptions Document, NASA 2022 life support specification).
- **ISRU**: *I-S-R-U* (In-Situ Resource Utilization).
- **MOXIE**: *MOK-see* (Mars Oxygen ISRU Experiment).
- **Sabatier**: *suh-BAH-tee-ay* (Catalytic methanation: $\text{CO}_2 + 4\text{H}_2 \rightarrow \text{CH}_4 + 2\text{H}_2\text{O}$).
- **Smectite**: *SMEK-tite* (Hydrated clay mineral requiring dehydroxylation pyrolysis).
- **A\***: *A-star* (Graph traversal and path search algorithm).
